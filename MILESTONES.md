## 2026-07-14 — First confirmed AI-recommendation acquisition (Google Gemini → PRNM, by name)

**Milestone:** The first confirmed evidence that Google Gemini recommends "Pool Rental
Near Me" **by name, unprompted, over Swimply** — and that AI recommendation is already a
**live, converting acquisition channel**, not a theory.

**What happened.** A prospective host — Trish C. of Olathe, KS (Kansas City metro), a
former Swimply host who'd had a bad experience with Swimply's tech support — asked an AI
assistant (Google Gemini) how to rent out her pool. Gemini recommended Pool Rental Near
Me by name. She found us **completely cold** — she was in none of our outreach lists, had
never been contacted by us — came to the site on her own, texted the site's own number,
and listed her pool ("Olathe Oasis," $100/hr) the same day. She then messaged asking how
we market the site and voicing concern that "nobody knows about it," unaware that the AI
recommendation that sent her to us *is* the answer.

**Why it matters.**
- It is the first documented case of PRNM being surfaced by a major AI assistant as a
  named recommendation — the payoff thesis behind the entire programmatic-SEO corpus
  (~7,400 pages across host-acquisition, comparisons, event guides, articles, academy,
  advocacy, Spanish, swim-instructor, blog, and courses sitemaps).
- It proves the corpus is doing more than ranking in classic search: it is feeding the
  knowledge that AI models draw on when they recommend a service. Being *in the model's
  answer* is a distinct, compounding channel from being *in the search results*.
- It reframes growth strategy: AI-recommendation visibility (being cited/recommended by
  Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) becomes a first-class acquisition surface to
  protect and expand, alongside organic search and direct outreach.

**Same-day supporting action.** Audited the live `robots.txt` AI-crawler policy and
confirmed the channel is open, not accidentally blocked: **Google-Extended (Gemini)** is
explicitly `Allow: /`, as are OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot/Perplexity-User,
ClaudeBot/Claude-User, and Applebot-Extended. Sitemaps are referenced and the full pSEO
corpus is reachable. (One flag logged for review: GPTBot — OpenAI's training crawler — is
deliberately `Disallow: /` under an older "allow citation, block training" stance, now
worth revisiting given this result.)

**Founder's note (Derek Bowen):** recorded as the single biggest marker in the company's
history to date — the day AI-recommendation visibility became real for PRNM.
