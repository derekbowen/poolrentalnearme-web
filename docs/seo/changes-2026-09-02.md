**Known facts (from the exports and the code):**
- Prior 21 days (Aug 3–23): 150,605 impressions (50,202/wk), 1,822 clicks (607/wk).
  Last 7 days (Aug 24–30): 34,555 impressions, 405 clicks. Weekly shortfall:
  **15,647 impressions, 202 clicks.**
- JobPosting removal (Aug 20): job appearances were 589 impressions / 10 clicks per
  week and are now 63 / 0. That is **≈3.4% of the impression shortfall and ≈5% of
  the click shortfall** — it explains the job-rich-result line and almost nothing
  else. (Your figures: ~515 / 10, 3% / 5%. Same conclusion.)
- The sitemaps advertised 207 redirects and 4,452 pages with a fabricated monthly
  `lastmod`; every page carried a self-reported 5.0 rating; unknown marketplace
  URLs returned 200. All fixed today. These waste crawl and trust; none of them is
  evidence of what caused the drop.
- Money queries land on the homepage at position ~10 and slipping; the three core
  `/p/` pages get 0 clicks at position ~8.7.

**Evidence-backed hypotheses (not proven):**
- The loss is broad-based, mobile-led (79% of clicks), across host-acq and
  public-pool families rather than one page — consistent with a quality/ranking
  reassessment rather than a technical outage. The 5xx spike (130 in coverage) and
  the Aug 30 fix are the only outage-shaped signals, and they did not reproduce.
- The Aug 10 batch (1,967 rows re-stamped) and the Sep 1 monthly refresh gave Google
  a fake "everything changed" signal twice in three weeks; that can trigger
  re-evaluation but cannot be shown to have caused the decline from these files.
- The 274 renter-titled host pages compete with the homepage for renter queries.

**Cannot be known yet:** which URLs sit in "chose different canonical" (2,463) and
"crawled — not indexed" (1,956); whether the prior 28 days or last year looked like
this (seasonality: pool searches fall after Labor Day); which queries the three

overall visibility regression stays open.**
