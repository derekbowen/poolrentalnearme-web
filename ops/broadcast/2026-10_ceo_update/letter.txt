Hi everyone,

I want to be brutally honest with the people who helped us build Pool Rental Near Me.

I’m a truck driver. I’ve funded and built much of this company while working long days on the road because I believed there was room for a different kind of pool-rental marketplace.

We listened to what hosts complained about and tried to actually solve those problems.

We eliminated host fees. Hosts keep 100% of their rental price.

We spent a tremendous amount of time putting real marketplace insurance in place.

We built the technology, mobile apps, payment infrastructure, host tools, SEO footprint, and the foundation of a national marketplace.

And we have built real supply.

But I’m also not going to pretend everything has gone according to plan.

Our supply has fluctuated. Hosts come and go. Customer support in a two-sided marketplace is far more complicated and expensive than it looks from the outside. Sometimes one issue can damage a relationship that took months to build.

I understand parts of this business today that I did not understand when I started it.

There is a reason established marketplaces build systems around communication, expectations, cancellations, availability, disputes, and support. Those problems have to be designed out of the marketplace wherever possible rather than solved manually every time they happen.

We have solved a lot of them.

We still have more to solve.

My belief has been that getting Pool Rental Near Me to roughly 1,000 active pools could fundamentally change the marketplace. At that point, geographic coverage, renter selection, SEO, repeat bookings, and marketplace liquidity begin looking very different.

But that is still a hypothesis that needs to be proven.

We also lost an investor this year, and the business is not currently producing the level of marketplace revenue or growth that I believe it needs to produce.

So rather than continue throwing my own money at every problem and assuming the answer is another piece of software, another marketing tool, or another check, I want to try something different.

I’m looking for someone who wants to run Pool Rental Near Me in 2027.

Not an employee who waits for me to tell them what to do.

I’m looking for a CEO/operator who can look at what we have built, challenge my assumptions, develop their own plan, and take responsibility for creating meaningful growth.

Maybe I have been looking at one of the problems incorrectly.

Maybe our biggest constraint is supply.

Maybe it is conversion.

Maybe it is the product.

Maybe it is host activation.

Maybe it is positioning.

Maybe it is marketplace liquidity.

Maybe we need capital.

Maybe we don’t.

I want somebody capable of figuring that out.

This would initially be an equity-based opportunity, with ownership, vesting, responsibilities, authority, milestones, and long-term compensation negotiated with the right person.

I would remain the founder and a significant shareholder, but I am willing to step back from operating the company and let somebody with a different skill set take the wheel.

And I mean actually take the wheel.

I need to see meaningful growth during 2027, not another year of activity that looks impressive but doesn’t move the business.

The person I’m looking for probably has experience in some combination of marketplaces, growth, sales, partnerships, product, SEO, fundraising, operations, or building companies without unlimited capital.

More importantly, they need to be resourceful.

If your first solution to every problem is, “Let’s spend another $100,000,” this probably isn’t the opportunity for you.

We have already built a considerable amount of infrastructure. The challenge now is turning those assets into a marketplace that compounds.

Many of you receiving this email are our founding hosts. You took a chance on Pool Rental Near Me before most people knew we existed, which is why I wanted you to hear about this first.

Some of you also run businesses, build companies, work in technology, marketing, real estate, hospitality, or other industries.

One of you may be exactly the person I’m looking for.

Or you may know them.

If you’re interested, don’t send me a polished corporate résumé and a bunch of buzzwords.

Send me something much more useful:

Tell me what you think I’m doing wrong.

Tell me what you think Pool Rental Near Me is doing right.

Tell me what you would change.

Tell me how you would get us to 1,000 active pools.

Tell me how you would turn those pools into bookings and revenue.

And tell me what you believe you could accomplish if I handed you the keys for the next year.

A little competitive desire to prove the bigger guys aren’t unbeatable probably wouldn’t hurt either.

If that sounds interesting, reply directly to this email.

Derek Bowen
Founder
