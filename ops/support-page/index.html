Questions

Is supporting PRNM required?

No. Supporting is voluntary.

How much should I contribute?

We suggest $20 a month, but $10 or $5 is welcome. Choose what works for your budget.

What do I receive?

At $20 a month, you receive customer service for account, listing, and booking questions. At $5 or $10 a month, your contribution supports the platform and does not include customer service. Every contribution helps fund the platform’s ongoing operations and improvements.

Is this $240 charged once a year?

No. At the suggested amount, you pay $20 each month. The $240 figure is the total over 12 months if you remain subscribed.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Email support@poolrentalnearme.com from the email you paid with and future charges stop. Your support stays active through the end of the period you have already paid for.

Why ask for monthly support?

The platform has ongoing costs even between bookings. Monthly support helps fund that work more predictably.

Is this a charitable donation or an investment?

No. This is a voluntary paid supporter membership for Pool Rental Near Me. Payments are taken by 10000 Solutions LLC. It does not provide equity, investment returns, or guaranteed bookings.

Does supporting PRNM include insurance?

Becoming a supporter does not itself create insurance coverage. Contact PRNM for the current protection terms applicable to your hosting activity.
